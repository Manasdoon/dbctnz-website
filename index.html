Who We Are

Serving the Indian Diaspora in Aotearoa

Devbhoomi Charitable Trust NZ is one of New Zealand's most established Indian community organisations — over a decade of service through cultural celebration, community welfare, and meaningful connection. From being the first in New Zealand to celebrate Uttarakhand State Foundation Day, to delivering government programmes for the Ministry of Education, the Trust has earned recognition at the highest levels of civic and political life in Aotearoa.

Our Work

What We Do

Four pillars of service connecting the Indian diaspora with community, culture, and Aotearoa.

Community Welfare

Funeral support, settlement consulting, youth programmes, and everyday outreach for families across New Zealand.

Cultural Diplomacy

Pioneering Uttarakhand State Foundation Day celebrations and Mahakumbh promotion recognised nationally.

Education & Civic

Formal facilitator for the Ministry of Education. Trusted across all political parties for diaspora engagement.

Arts & Culture

Hosting celebrated performers and creating platforms where culture bridges communities old and new.
