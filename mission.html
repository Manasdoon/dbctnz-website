Our Mission

Why we exist, what we stand for, and the commitments we've made to the communities we serve across Aotearoa.

Our Mission

To connect and support the Indian diaspora across Aotearoa New Zealand — through community welfare, cultural celebration, and civic engagement — while building genuine bridges between the people of Dev Bhoomi and the people of Aotearoa.

Our Vision

A thriving, connected Indian diaspora in New Zealand — one that preserves its cultural roots while actively contributing to, and being embraced by, the wider Aotearoa community. We look to a future where welfare, culture, civic life, and economic opportunity reinforce one another — where community members and businesses prosper together, and where the Trust remains a trusted bridge between communities and government.

Our Values

Service without discrimination, transparency in how we operate, respect for every faith and background, and a genuine commitment to the wellbeing of families navigating life in a new country. We work across all political parties and faiths, guided by community need rather than self-interest.

How We Deliver On This

Our Charitable Purposes

As registered with the New Zealand Charities Register — the practical commitments behind our mission.

Cultural & Spiritual Life

Conducting events that build understanding of Hindu philosophy, and celebrating festivals that promote harmony between communities.

Community Welfare

Volunteering with the local community and supporting the needy — including disaster relief, refugees, and migrants finding their feet in a new country.

Health & Financial Literacy

Promoting public health through education and counselling, alongside financial and digital literacy for families settling in New Zealand.

Sustainability & Education

Promoting sustainable living and efficient waste management, and providing sponsorship and support for academic achievement.

Economic Opportunity & Prosperity

Helping community members and businesses connect and grow — including through our community business directory — so that prosperity within the diaspora strengthens the wider community too.
