Who We Are

Serving the Indian Diaspora in Aotearoa

Devbhoomi Charitable Trust NZ is one of New Zealand's most established Indian community organisations — over a decade of service through cultural celebration, community welfare, and meaningful connection. From being the first in New Zealand to celebrate Uttarakhand State Foundation Day, to delivering government programmes for the Ministry of Education, the Trust has earned recognition at the highest levels of civic and political life in Aotearoa.

The Trust is a registered charitable trust incorporated under the Charitable Trusts Act, operating nationwide across New Zealand. Our work spans religious and cultural activities, community development, and support services for the general public — with a particular focus on the wellbeing of the Indian and Uttarakhand diaspora communities.
