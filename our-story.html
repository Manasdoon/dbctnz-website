Our Story

A decade of service,
community, and connection

Devbhoomi Charitable Trust NZ has been a consistent, trusted presence for the Indian diaspora across Aotearoa — through life's most significant moments and its grandest celebrations alike.

01 · Community Welfare

Putting people first, every day

From the moment families arrive in New Zealand, the Trust has been there — helping them navigate the practical and emotional challenges of life in a new country. Our welfare support spans funeral services, settlement consulting, and everyday community outreach that meets people where they are.

"A community is only as strong as the support it offers its most vulnerable members."

Our youth programmes create pathways for the next generation to stay connected to their cultural roots while thriving in New Zealand society. Through cultural events, festivals, and gatherings, the Trust has brought communities together in celebration of shared heritage for over a decade.

02 · Cultural Diplomacy

Pioneering a milestone recognised at the highest levels

Devbhoomi Charitable Trust holds the distinction of being the first organisation in New Zealand to celebrate Uttarakhand State Foundation Day — a milestone acknowledged at the highest levels of government in India.

The occasion was graced with congratulatory addresses from the Honourable Chief Minister and Governor of Uttarakhand. In keeping with the Prime Minister's national initiative to promote millets, the Trust prepared food exclusively from a diverse variety of Himalayan millets — a gesture that was both culturally resonant and globally relevant.

"The first in New Zealand to bring Uttarakhand State Foundation Day to Aotearoa."

03 · India on the World Stage

Bringing rarely told histories to New Zealand audiences

Subsequent Foundation Day celebrations have grown in stature and depth, featuring formal exhibitions on Uttarakhand's contribution to India's freedom movement and its proud legacy in the First and Second World Wars — stories that have rarely been told to New Zealand audiences.

These events earned formal notes of thanks from the Minister for Culture and Tourism, reflecting the quality and significance of the Trust's work on the national stage.

"The Trust's Mahakumbh promotion was recognised and shared by the Ministry of Ethnic Communities on their official platforms."

The Trust also championed the promotion of Mahakumbh — one of the world's largest spiritual gatherings — through cultural programming that reached well beyond the Indian community.

04 · Bridge Between Communities

A trusted facilitator across civic and political life

Devbhoomi Charitable Trust serves as a formal facilitator for the Ministry of Education, delivering key programmes to the Indian diaspora and ensuring that government services reach those who need them most.

The Trust also plays a valued role in New Zealand's democratic process, working across all political parties as a trusted facilitator to create meaningful dialogue with the Indian community ahead of elections.

"Trusted by all sides of the political spectrum to build genuine dialogue with the Indian community."

05 · Arts & Culture

Culture as the bridge between communities

Beyond civic work, the Trust has championed Indian arts and culture in New Zealand — hosting celebrated singers and performers, and creating platforms where culture becomes the bridge between communities old and new.

From Diwali celebrations to classical performances, the Trust has consistently created spaces where the richness of Uttarakhand and Indian heritage is shared with all of Aotearoa — not just the diaspora.

"Creating platforms where culture becomes the bridge between communities."
